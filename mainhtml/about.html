Rovelore Chronicles

Rovelore Chronicles is an interactive educational web application developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, inspired by the challenge “Abandoned but Not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars.”

Our project brings the stories of abandoned and forgotten space exploration equipment to life through interactive storytelling. From Mars rovers to lunar instruments, every piece of equipment has a unique journey filled with scientific discoveries, engineering achievements, and challenges.

Through an engaging and child-friendly experience, Rovelore Chronicles allows young explorers to discover these machines, learn about their scientific contributions, and understand why they were left behind.

What Can You Explore?

Interactive Planet Exploration

Discover Mars and the Moon through interactive environments filled with exploration markers.

Forgotten Equipment Stories

Select different abandoned NASA equipment and uncover the stories behind their missions.

Scientific Discoveries

Learn about their hardware, objectives, achievements, and contributions to space exploration.

Educational Storytelling

Understand complex space exploration missions through simple, engaging, and child-friendly explanations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make space exploration history more accessible, interesting, and meaningful for young learners.

Instead of presenting forgotten equipment as outdated machines, we want children to see them as explorers that helped humanity take another step toward understanding the universe.

By combining interactive technology with educational storytelling, we hope to turn curiosity into knowledge and knowledge into inspiration.

Our Vision

We believe that an explorer does not become insignificant simply because its mission has ended.

The machines left behind on Mars and the Moon represent years of human innovation, scientific ambition, and curiosity. Although their missions may be over, their contributions remain an important part of space exploration history.

Our vision is to transform these forgotten machines into meaningful stories that preserve their scientific legacy, connect younger generations with the history of space exploration, and inspire future scientists, engineers, and explorers around the world.

Beyond the Web Experience

Rovelore Chronicles has the potential to become more than an educational website. We envision using this platform as a bridge between space exploration history and interactive learning experiences worldwide.

Planetarium Integration

Introduce Rovelore Chronicles into planetariums and educational institutions, allowing children to experience the stories of forgotten space explorers through immersive presentations and audio storytelling.

Global Reach

Make these stories accessible to children worldwide, connecting students from different countries with NASA's space exploration history through an engaging educational platform.

Multimedia Learning Experiences

Expand the platform through audio, visual presentations, and interactive content to help children connect more deeply with the journeys, discoveries, and scientific contributions of abandoned space equipment.

Through these possibilities, we hope to turn forgotten space exploration history into a shared learning experience that reaches beyond borders and inspires the next generation of explorers.
