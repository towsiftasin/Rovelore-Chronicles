# Rovelore Chronicles
**[Visit the Live Website](https://rovelore-chronicles.vercel.app)**
# Project Summary

Rovelore Chronicles is an interactive storytelling platform that brings the stories of NASA's abandoned and retired equipment on the Moon and Mars to life.

Space exploration is filled with machines that have completed their missions, lost communication, or been left behind on other worlds. Although these explorers are no longer active, their journeys contributed valuable discoveries and expanded our understanding of the Solar System.

Rovelore Chronicles transforms these missions into engaging, accessible, and visually driven stories designed especially for curious students and young learners.

Through an immersive exploration experience, users can discover abandoned equipment across extraterrestrial landscapes and follow their individual stories—from launch and mission objectives to discoveries, challenges, and eventual abandonment.

The project aims to bridge the gap between complex space-mission information and student-friendly learning by turning scientific history into an experience that is easier to explore, understand, and remember.

«Every abandoned machine has a story. Rovelore Chronicles brings those stories back to life.»

# Objectives

- Make NASA's space-exploration history more accessible to students.
- Present complex mission information through simple storytelling.
- Create an engaging alternative to traditional text-heavy educational resources.
- Highlight the scientific contributions of equipment that is no longer operational.
- Encourage curiosity and interest in planetary science and space exploration.

# Key Features

- Interactive Planetary Exploration — Explore environments inspired by the Moon and Mars.
- Equipment Discovery — Discover abandoned or retired NASA equipment across the surface.
- Mission Stories — Learn the journey and purpose behind each explorer.
- Visual Storytelling — Combine visuals, animations, and narrative to make information engaging.
- Student-Friendly Learning — Present scientific information in an accessible format.

# Technology

# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

Rovelore Chronicles is developed as a project for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, inspired by the challenge:

“Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA’s Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars.”

# Vision

Rovelore Chronicles is built around a simple idea:

An explorer does not become insignificant simply because its mission has ended.

The machines left behind on other worlds represent years of engineering, scientific ambition, discovery, and human curiosity. By telling their stories, we can help a new generation understand not only what these machines achieved, but also why their journeys matter.
